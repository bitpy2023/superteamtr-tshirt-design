## 2. Recommendation — Concept 1, *Arena Protocol*, developed

**Why Concept 1 wins**

1. **Brand relevance.** The brief's core idea — a Colosseum-style arena where
   the Superteam community gathers and competes — is expressed *structurally*
   (real arcade tiers, gates, arena floor, spectator ring, one highlighted
   builder on the floor). Nothing has to be explained.
2. **Originality.** It is a plan view of a stadium wired as a network: the
   Colosseum reference is abstract, top-down and contemporary, not an
   illustration of ruins. No existing artwork, poster or hackathon graphic
   resembles this construction.
3. **Wearability + impact.** It has the strongest silhouette at distance, the
   cleanest close-up craft, and the best hierarchy: arena → SuperteamTR →
   tagline → technical detail.
4. **Scalability.** One emblem becomes: 320 × 400 mm back print, 90 × 104 mm
   front chest mark, 40 mm sleeve mark, neck label, and a 40–60 mm embroidery
   adaptation — every physical product in the SuperteamTR merch plan.
5. **Print feasibility.** Four inks with a real separation set, a 2-ink print
   build and a 1-ink fallback, all generated and delivered.

**The other two are not wasted** — they are delivered as alternates and they are
production-ready: Concept 2 is the low-cost screen-print and poster direction
(1–2 inks), Concept 3 is the embroidery / daily-wear / merch-extension direction.
A three-piece capsule (back print + back sheet + chest seal) is a credible
SuperteamTR drop, and all three files ship in this package.
