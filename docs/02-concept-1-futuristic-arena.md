# 02 · Concept 1 — *ARENA PROTOCOL* (futuristic arena)

> **One-sentence idea:** The Colosseum rebuilt as a live Web3 stadium seen from
> above — a two-tier arcade ring around a Solana core, wired like a network and
> measured like an architectural drawing.

*Master file:* `design/concepts/concept-1-futuristic-arena.svg` (320 × 400 mm)
*Mockup:* `mockups/concept-1-mockup-back.png`

## Visual story

You are looking straight down into a futuristic arena. Two tiers of arched
bays — the Colosseum's arcades, reduced to pure geometry — step inward toward a
brighter inner ring and an arena floor marked with seating spokes, an arena
rail and one highlighted *player* marker (a builder on the floor). At the exact
centre sits the official Solana logomark inside a white "core coin": the
protocol at the middle of the ring, the thing everybody in the arena is
building on. Outside the wall, the structure becomes data: a node ring,
a dashed signal band, a telemetry tick ring, dimension marks and read-out bars.
A dashed East–West axis runs through the whole figure like a bridge — the two
terminus blocks in brand red.

## Recommended T-shirt colour

Washed black (`#17171A`) — the arena needs a dark void to read as architecture.
Secondary: bone / off-white.

## Main graphic structure

| Layer | Element | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Outer | frame ring with two deliberate "glitch" breaks + red index squares | 0.55 mm |
| Data | telemetry tick ring (72 ticks, every 6th long) | 0.34–0.42 mm |
| Network | dashed signal band + 12 node capsules with leaders | 0.35–0.70 mm |
| Architecture | main arcade: 24 arched bays, open to the background (real openings, not filled) | 0.9–1.0 mm walls, 6.5 mm bays |
| Architecture | second tier: 16 arched bays, stepped inward | 0.85–0.95 mm |
| Floor | seating ring, 16 spokes, gate rail with 4 red index nodes, player marker | 0.40–0.60 mm |
| Core | white coin + red hairline ring + official Solana logomark | – |
| Brand | official SuperteamTR lockup (largest brand shape), official Solana logotype | – |
| Type | tagline "THE ARENA FOR BUILDERS" (display), header row data, footer micro-line | – |

## Typography direction

* **Anton** — condensed display, set as a wide, tightly tracked wordmark for
  `THE ARENA FOR BUILDERS`; caps-only, optical tracking +9 %.
* **JetBrains Mono** — technical micro-type: `ISTANBUL · TR`,
  `41.0082° N / 28.9784° E`, `CONNECT. CREATE. SHIP.` (tracked +34…42 %).
* All type in English, outlined, minimum cap height 3.2 mm at print size.
* The headline deliberately sets the shirt's three messages in reading order:
  arena → SuperteamTR → build together.

## Colour palette

White `#FFFFFF` · SuperteamTR red `#D6223B` · Solana purple `#9945FF` ·
Solana teal `#28E0B9` · Solana green `#14F195` (gradient only) — on washed black.

## Logo placement

* **SuperteamTR lockup** — 202 × 52.9 mm, centred, directly under the arena
  (dominant brand shape). Monochrome white build for the dark garment.
* **Solana logomark** — 33.6 × 29.3 mm, centred inside the arena core, full
  official gradient.
* **Solana logotype** — 82 × 12.2 mm, centred at the foot of the print, closing
  the composition and answering the SuperteamTR lockup at the top.

## Print method recommendation

* **First choice:** DTG or DTF at 320 × 400 mm (holds the Solana gradient
  exactly, no screens to burn, ideal for limited runs).
* **Screen print:** 4 inks — white underbase, TR red, Solana purple, Solana teal
  (the gradient becomes a hard-stop two-band wash); separations supplied.
  Or 2-ink print build (white + green) if the budget is tight.
* **Embroidery:** the arena core alone, 40–60 mm, on chest or sleeve.

## Why it fits SuperteamTR

The arena *is* the Superteam promise: a place where builders gather, compete,
and ship together — while the Solana core makes the ecosystem relationship
explicit and the red accents and Istanbul coordinates quietly say *Türkiye*
without a single flag.

## Strengths / risks

**Strengths** — instantly recognisable silhouette; the arcade reads as
architecture from 5 m and as data up close; strong front/back hierarchy; works
in 4, 2 and 1 ink; scales from 320 mm down to a 40 mm sleeve mark.

**Risks & mitigation**
* *Busy centre* → the arena floor is deliberately open (no interior die-cut at
  body centre) and the coin is the only fully solid element.
* *Gradient on press* → documented 2-colour and 1-colour builds; the two brand
  purples/greens are Pantone-matched on press.
* *Arches too fine on coarse knits* → bay walls are 0.9 mm minimum and bays are
  6.5 mm wide, above every ink-bleed threshold; a coarser-knit variant can be
  generated by reducing `arch_count` to 16 in `tools/build_artwork.py`.
