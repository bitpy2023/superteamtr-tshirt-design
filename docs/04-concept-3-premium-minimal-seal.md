# 04 · Concept 3 — *THE SEAL* (premium minimal emblem)

> **One-sentence idea:** A single refined roundel: the official SuperteamTR
> monogram crowned by eight arcade bays, locked up with the wordmark, the Solana
> logotype and one red accent — a seal, not a poster.

*Master file:* `design/concepts/concept-3-premium-minimal-seal.svg` (300 × 300 mm)
*Mockup:* `mockups/concept-3-mockup-front.png`

## Visual story

One circle, held in the middle of the chest. Inside it: the official
SuperteamTR monogram (crescent and star intact) over a small wordmark, the
Solana logotype carrying the only colour in the composition, and a ring of eight
arched bays — the arena reduced to a single architectural gesture. The outer
band carries circular micro-type (`SOLANA ECOSYSTEM` above, `* BUILD TOGETHER *`
below) and two red dots on the horizontal axis: the East–West bridge reference
compressed to its simplest possible form.

## Recommended T-shirt colour

Washed black (`#17171A`); also bone, and asphalt/charcoal. Works on a hoodie or
cap with no changes.

## Main graphic structure

| Layer | Element |
|---|---|
| Rings | outer 1.3 mm frame, inner hairline ring |
| Architecture | 8 arched bays (the only structural motif), 45° apart |
| Type ring | circular micro-type top and bottom, red dots at 3 and 9 o'clock |
| Centre | official SuperteamTR monogram (76 mm wide) |
| Lockup | `SuperteamTR` wordmark 17 mm cap, official Solana logotype 58 mm |
| Footer | `ISTANBUL · TR` micro-line |

## Typography direction

* **Space Grotesk 700** for `SUPERTEAMTR` — set wide and confident; it is the
  only large type in the design.
* **JetBrains Mono 500** for the circular type ring and footer; tracking chosen
  so the roundel closes precisely (measured, not eyeballed: the text is placed
  on a true circle, not a distortion of a straight line).
* No display face at all — restraint is the concept.

## Colour palette

White `#FFFFFF` + SuperteamTR red `#D6223B` + official Solana gradient on the
logotype. Two inks on press; one ink on request.

## Logo placement

* **SuperteamTR monogram** — 76 mm, centred, dominant.
* **Solana logotype** — 58 × 8.6 mm, centred under the wordmark, full colour.
* No third mark; the roundel itself is the brand frame.

## Print method recommendation

* **Embroidery or patch** — the roundel is designed for it: solid monogram,
  thick bays, no hairlines below 1 mm.
* **Screen print, 1–2 inks** on chest (90 mm), left chest (60 mm), sleeve (40 mm).
* Also ideal for **DTF transfers** on the small chest mark and for **laser-etched
  leather/wood event tags** from the same vector.

## Why it fits SuperteamTR

Superteams are communities with a badge. This is the badge: no arguments, no
campaign, just the mark of the Turkish chapter, wearable under a jacket — the
shirt a builder puts on for a pitch meeting or a flight, not only for a
hackathon.

## Strengths / risks

**Strengths** — highest wearability and the strongest "premium streetwear"
signal; cheapest and fastest to produce; scales down to 30 mm without loss; the
easiest concept to extend into a caps/hoodie/sticker family.

**Risks & mitigation**
* *Might read as "just a logo"* → the eight arches and the circular micro-type
  carry enough craft to avoid that; on the flat lay the roundel reads as a
  designed seal, not a printed logo.
* *Circular type is easy to get wrong* → type is set on true circles with
  per-glyph rotation (see `Shaper.circular_path`), verified at 400 % zoom.
* *Small type on the ring* → the ring type is 6.2 mm cap at chest size and is
  supplementary; nothing essential is below 8 pt.
